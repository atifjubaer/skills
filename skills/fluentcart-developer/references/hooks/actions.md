# FluentCart Action Hooks

Action hooks allow you to execute custom code at specific points in FluentCart's execution flow. They are perfect for triggering side effects, sending notifications, logging events, or performing custom business logic.

**Categories:**

1. [Orders](./actions/orders.md) - Order lifecycle, payments, shipping, refunds
2. [Subscriptions](./actions/subscriptions.md) - Subscription lifecycle, renewals, reminders
3. [Licenses](./actions/licenses.md) - License management, activation, site management
4. [Cart & Checkout](./actions/cart-and-checkout.md) - Shopping flow from cart to completion
5. [Customers & Users](./actions/customers-and-users.md) - User and customer management
6. [Products & Coupons](./actions/products-and-coupons.md) - Catalog management
7. [Payments & Integrations](./actions/payments-and-integrations.md) - Payment gateways and third-party integrations
8. [Admin & Templates](./actions/admin-and-templates.md) - Frontend rendering and administrative functions
9. [Modules](./actions/modules.md) - MCP server and advanced product variations
10. [Pro Modules](./actions/pro-modules.md) Pro - Software releases, Paddle webhooks, saved payment methods, MCP
